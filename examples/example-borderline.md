# Example: borderline (fabricated)

## Input
Results: Of 540 invited pupils, 312 (58%) completed the questionnaire. In the mixed model with a random intercept for school (Table 3), wellbeing scores were higher in the intervention group. Overall, 45% of respondents agreed that the programme was useful.

## Expected output
**Light: GREEN**

**Flags**
None.

**Check manually**
- Methods described in the results: "In the mixed model with a random intercept for school (Table 3)" names the model structure. This probably labels the analysis rather than explaining it, but the reader may want to decide.
- Percentage without denominator: "45% of respondents" most likely refers to the 312 who responded, but this is not stated explicitly.

**Send-back message**
Not needed.

**Scope**
This screening detects basic reporting issues from a fixed list. It does not assess whether the analysis or conclusions are correct.

## Why this example is here
It shows that doubtful cases go under "Check manually" and do not turn the light red.
