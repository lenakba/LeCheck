# Example: red (fabricated)

## Input
Results: Of the participants, 62% reported improved sleep. A one-way ANOVA was used to compare the three groups, and the result was significant. Group C had the highest mean score.

## Expected output
**Light: RED**

**Flags**
- Item: Number of participants missing
- Quote: No statement found (looked in the running text; no tables or captions provided)
- Why it matters: Readers cannot judge precision without the sample size.

- Item: Percentage without denominator
- Quote: "Of the participants, 62% reported improved sleep."
- Why it matters: The group the percentage refers to and its size are not stated.

- Item: Methods described in the results
- Quote: "A one-way ANOVA was used to compare the three groups, and the result was significant."
- Why it matters: Describing the test belongs in Methods.

**Check manually**
None.

**Send-back message**
These results were screened with a standardized AI-tool, and the AI flagged these issues. Before I read it in full, please:
1. State the number of participants analysed.
2. State the denominator for each percentage.
3. Move descriptions of statistical methods to the Methods section and keep the Results to the findings.

**Scope**
This screening detects basic reporting issues from a fixed list. It does not assess whether the analysis or conclusions are correct.
